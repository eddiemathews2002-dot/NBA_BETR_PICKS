NBA Betr Picks is an independent, interactive project demo. Explore player stats and build a lineup in three simple steps.

  1. Find your angle.Choose a matchup or stat category. Search for a player or save them to your watchlist.
  2. Make your picks.Select More or Less than the displayed line. Add 2–6 different players to your lineup.
  3. Save your lineup.Keep your practice picks in My lineups, right here in this browser.
All matchups, stat lines, and game results are fictional sample data, not current projections or recommendations. This demo has no wagers, payments, or real payouts. It is not affiliated with NBA or Betr. Player imagery: NBA.com. Picks and watchlists are stored only in this browser.
